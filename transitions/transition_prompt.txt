   Parameters : 1) tansition_dur - how many seconds to get to the next target "landmark"
   				2) freq - the frequency at the next target landmark
   				3) phase - the phase at the next target landmark

 The parameters can be set by a user of the object any time, but the soundmodel doesn't use them to affect the phasor rotoation speed until the "transition" event which causes the phasor to slowly move to the new freq and phase over the transitionion_dur:  
 
   	"Transition" event 
   		Triggers: 
   				current_freq -> freq over duration (transition_dur) (linearly for now)
   				current_phase -> phase over transition_dur)

   				The linearity of the frequency transition will, of course, have to be slightly adjusted (evenly distributed over duration, and as minimally as possible) so that the current_phase ends up at the phase exactly at the end of the duration.
